Privacy policy

Guesthouse tools: Guesthouse bookings and Mail assistant. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Who uses them

Both tools are used only by the owner of one small family business, a guesthouse with a taverna, with the business's own Google accounts. Guests and anyone else never use them and never sign in to them.

What Google data they access

Guesthouse bookings:

  • Google Calendar, only the calendars it creates (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created): it creates one secondary calendar in the owner's account and creates, changes and deletes its own events in it, one per stay. It cannot see any other calendar or event.

Mail assistant:

  • Gmail (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify): it reads the business mailbox, adds and removes its own labels, archives newsletters and creates reply drafts. It never sends, forwards or deletes mail, and never changes the mailbox settings or filters.
  • Google Drive, only its own files (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): the copies of the invoices received by email, in a folder it creates. It cannot see any other file in Drive.
  • Google Calendar, only the calendars it creates (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created): the owner's payment and table reminders.

How the data is used

Guesthouse bookings uses the data only to show the guesthouse's stays in the owner's own calendar. Mail assistant uses it only to sort the business mailbox, to prepare drafts that the owner reviews and sends himself, to keep copies of invoices and to remind the owner of payments, arrivals and table bookings. The sorting and the drafts are made by a language model that runs on the owner's own computers; no mail text is sent to any outside AI service. Neither tool uses the data for advertising, sells it, uses it to build profiles, or uses it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Guests' data

Bookings and emails hold the personal data of guests (for example name, dates of stay, phone number, email address). The business uses it only to manage the stay. It stays in the owner's own booking system, Gmail, Calendar and Drive and is deleted or made anonymous when it is no longer needed for the stay and for the business's legal obligations.

Where the data is kept

Guesthouse bookings: the stays are kept in the app's database on the owner's own server, which only the owner's own devices can reach; its access token is kept encrypted on the same server.

Mail assistant: it keeps no copy of the mails themselves, only message identifiers, its labels and its reminders, on the owner's own server, for 90 days; its access tokens are kept on the same server. The copies of the invoices are kept in the owner's own Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not shared with or transferred to anyone. No one outside the business reads it.

Keeping and deleting

A cancelled stay is deleted from the calendar as well. The calendars a tool created stay in the owner's account until the owner deletes them. Removing a tool's access in the Google Account, under Third-party apps and services, stops all further access by that tool at once.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by Guesthouse bookings and by Mail assistant adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, scripts or resources from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions go to the support email address shown on each tool's Google sign-in screen.
