Guesthouse tools

Two private tools of one small family business, a guesthouse with a taverna, use Google APIs. Only the owner of the business uses them, with the business's own Google accounts. They are not offered to anyone else.

Guesthouse bookings

Guesthouse bookings is the booking book of the guesthouse: the owner keeps the stays (room, dates, guests) in it, on the owner's own server. With the owner's consent it also writes one event per stay into a calendar of its own in the owner's Google Calendar, so that the owner sees the bookings on the phone.

  • Google Calendar, only the calendars this app creates: it makes one secondary calendar and adds, changes and removes its own events there.

It cannot see the owner's other calendars or events. It shares nothing with anyone.

Mail assistant

Mail assistant (being built) sorts the business mailbox for the owner: it adds labels, archives newsletters and writes reply drafts that the owner reads, edits and sends himself. It never sends an email. It keeps a copy of the invoices that arrive by email and reminds the owner of payments and table bookings.

  • Gmail: to read the business mailbox, add and remove its own labels, archive newsletters and create drafts. It never sends, forwards or deletes mail and never changes the mailbox settings.
  • Google Drive, only the files this tool creates: the copies of the invoices received by email.
  • Google Calendar, only the calendars this tool creates: the payment and table reminders.

The sorting and the drafts are made by a language model that runs on the owner's own computers: no mail text is sent to any outside AI service. It shares nothing with anyone.
